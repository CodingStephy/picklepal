Scoring, made simple
0 – 0 – 2

Three numbers, one story

Every pickleball score has three parts: your score, their score, and which server you are (1 or 2).

💡 “Zero–zero–two” just means: nobody has scored yet, and the second server is serving. That's how most games start!
🎯

Only the serving team scores

If you win a rally while receiving, you don't get a point — you get the serve. Points only come when your team served the rally.

💡 This is the #1 thing that confuses new players. You're ahead of the curve now.
📣

Call it before you serve

Say the score out loud before every serve: “4 – 2 – 1!” It keeps everyone synced and instantly makes you sound like a regular.

💡 Nervous about saying it wrong? Everyone corrects each other kindly. It's part of the game's culture.
Solo drill · 10 min
